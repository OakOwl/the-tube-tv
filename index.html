📖 HOW TO USE THE TUBE
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
FToggle fullscreen for the TV screen.
📺 The TV
⏻ POWERTurns the TV on or off. The set must be powered on before you can tune to a channel.
VOLAdjust playback volume.
STATICAdjusts the intensity of the channel-change static effect.
BRIGHTAdjusts the brightness of the screen.
⛶Click the fullscreen icon on the screen (or press F) to expand the picture.
📼 VHS Deck Controls
⏪ -15sRewind 15 seconds within the current episode.
▶ / ⏸Play or pause the current episode.
⏩ +15sFast-forward 15 seconds within the current episode.
🎛 Top Bar
DONATESupport the project via Buy Me a Coffee.
📬 UPDATESUse the bar at the top of the page to hear when new shows are added. Hid it? Bring the bar back.
SURPRISE MEJumps to a random show and episode — perfect for discovering something new.
LIVE MODETurns The Tube into a real "live channel" — the schedule decides what's on, just like real TV.
CATALOGUEBrowse every show in the archive, filter by category, and tune in directly.
🔄 TOGGLE SCHEDULEManually switch between Schedule A and B for testing.
SEARCHType in the box at the top of the guide to filter it by show name.
📋 Programme Guide & Episodes
CH LISTClick any channel in the guide to tune in — playback starts from that day's scheduled episode.
EP LISTUse the episode list (the Episodes tab on phones and tablets) to jump to any episode of the current show.
